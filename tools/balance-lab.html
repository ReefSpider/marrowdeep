髄Where Marrowdeep's scaling breaks

The level curve holds. Rarity doesn't. A typical rare kit kills an ordinary foe in about a second from monster level 1 to 100, but a unique kit does the same job 2 to 9 times faster and an abyssal weapon 11 to 38 times faster. Bad gear barely hurts; good gear removes the fight.

What multiplies

Every source of damage multiplies every other one, and the rarer items add sources on top of bigger numbers. These are the specific stacks, in the order they matter.

Flat damage types ignore item level on uniques

×4 – ×9 early

signatureElem always rolls the top tier of a damage type (9–16 fire, ×1.2 on uniques, ×1.5 on abyssals), even at item level 1. A level-1 weapon hits for about 7, so a unique's +15 fire per blow more than doubles it, and rings and amulets add more. This is the whole early-game blowout.

Abyssal items stack five boosts at once

×11 at every level

Enhanced Damage of 380–500% (rares top out at 130%), the best base of the class ×1.2, crit 25–45%, attack speed 20–30%, and a damage type at ×1.5. Each alone is a fair abyssal; together they are an order of magnitude.

Uniques get four boosts rares don't

×2 late

uniqueFloor (best base ×1.05), empower (stats ×1.2), two extra legendary-strength stats, and the signature damage type. v16 did this to stop uniques being weaker than legendaries; it overshot.

The Abyssal grade doubles a stat

up to ×2 on one line

A grade-4 roll lands between 1.5× and 2× the top of its range, so a 130% Enhanced Damage line can roll 260%. Rare, but it is what players remember.

Weapon class spread is wider than rarity

×4.7 between classes

At monster level 50 a rare great hammer averages 3,738 sheet DPS and a rare sickle 796. Within one rarity, the 90th-percentile kit has 3–4× the DPS of the 10th, mostly from the class it rolled. Reach, stun and dual wield make up some of this, but not ×4.7.

Defense has no level in its formula

20–60 hits to die

Damage taken is 100 / (100 + 2 × defense). Defense grows 5% per item level and with Enhanced Defense, so a rare kit already takes 20 ghoul hits to kill and a unique kit 40–60. Monster damage can't catch up because it isn't part of the sum.

How other games keep this in check

A budget per item level. In World of Warcraft an item's level and slot set the total stats it can carry, and each stat has a price against that budget. Rarity changes how the budget is spent, not how big it is by orders of magnitude. Stat budget

Fewer stats, fewer conditional multipliers, hard caps. Diablo IV's Season 4 cut rares to 2 affixes and legendaries to 3, removed many conditional affixes, and capped armour at 85% physical reduction regardless of monster level; power moved into crafting steps players control. Season 4 summary

Additive pools and defence that depends on the hit. Path of Exile adds every "increased" bonus into one pool and keeps the multiplicative "more" bonuses rare, so stacking one more % stat gains less each time. Armour reduces a hit by armour ÷ (armour + 5 × hit), so it weakens against bigger monsters. Armour

A target curve and a simulator. The common thread: designers write down target time-to-kill and hits-to-die per level, simulate typical, bad and best gear against it, and treat any change that leaves the band as a regression. That's what BAL.run() now does for Marrowdeep.

Proposed fix

Targets, as multiples of a current rare kit's sheet DPS: stale gear ×0.5, magic ×0.75, rare ×1, legendary ×1.25, unique ×1.5, abyssal ×1.9. A lucky roll (90th percentile) adds at most a further ×1.3.

  1. Roll unique and abyssal damage types at the item level's tier, like every other stat, and scale flat damage types with the weapon's own damage instead of a fixed table.
  2. Give abyssal and unique items a damage budget: keep their identities (crit, speed, a type) but cut Enhanced Damage to fit the ×1.5 and ×1.9 targets. Drop the extra ×1.2 from empower.
  3. Make the Abyssal grade +25% over the top of a range, not up to +100%.
  4. Pull weapon classes to within ±25% of each other by sheet DPS at equal hands, crediting reach and stun.
  5. Put monster level into defense: reduction = defense ÷ (defense + 40 × monster level), capped at 75%.
  6. Smooth the Pilgrim → Ashen seam so the first Ashen level is no easier than the Count's.
  7. Run BAL.run() after every balance change; any profile outside its band fails.
