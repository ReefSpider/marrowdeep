Act I · kiro · the way home

The Road Home

Cold and grey-green under a white sky: rain, fog, mud. The red Wound sits faint on the horizon and grows as he gets closer to home.

"He comes to the house now. The children ask for you. Come home."

The Wound is the one red thing in every Act I board. It is a smudge on the grasslands horizon and fills the sky over the border fort, so the player can read the distance home off the sky.

Act II · yami · darkness

Hollowmere in Darkness

Night city: torchlight, black banners, red only from the sky and the tithe-lanterns.

Act II never jump-scares. It never lets the player relax.

Most of Act II’s unease comes from design and sound, not art: townsfolk stop working and watch you pass; faces repeat and smiles are slightly too wide; bells ring at the wrong times; the ambient music is detuned a few cents; shadows point the wrong way; Cassian’s portrait is on every wall and its eyes track you; friendly villagers stay friendly until you’re close.

Lock the palettes

Each board’s chips are the proposed 6–8 colour palette for that region, plus the Wound red. Change any chip before tiles and parallax layers are made from it.

Prove the swamp in-engine

Build the swamp end to end (backdrop layers, tiles, two foes) and look at it running before generating the other regions.

Then generate

Run each prompt in SpriteCook or Blueprint Studio once their tools load in a session, keeping the palette hexes in the prompt. Save as art/moodboard/<region>.png.
