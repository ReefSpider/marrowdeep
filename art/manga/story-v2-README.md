# Marrowdeep story-v2 artwork

Completed all 60 individually listed storyboard panels and 8 reference sheets.

- Prologue: 25 panels.
- Act I: 16 panels.
- Act II: 19 panels.
- 53 generated panels, 7 reused from existing game art.
- Captions and sound effects are metadata, not painted into the artwork.
- All PNGs retain generator output dimensions; the catalog records target display ratios for the game's crop.
- Odo's hounds panel uses an exhausted seated master after an initial generation rejection; the loyalty beat remains.
- Art is registered in art/manga/manifest.json; this upload does not change the game's scene scripts.

## Source limitation

The supplied PDF has 20 pages and its detailed panel tables stop at Act II. It mentions 94 panels in its summary but individually specifies only 60. Act III and ending panel specifications are not in this copy. No missing panel IDs or captions have been invented.
